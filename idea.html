More on our idea

The thinking behind LEAP.

LEAP started with a simple question: what would it look like if the communities of our region worked together the way nations do when they share a goal?

Think of it as a United Nations for our region.

The nations of the world differ in language, history, and belief, and they keep those differences. But they share universal values and common problems. So they come to one table, stand up for what they hold in common, and take on together what none of them can solve as well alone.

LEAP is that table for the young leaders of Northern Virginia.

Like the UN
Members differ, and stay different.
They share values and problems.
Working together makes each of them more effective.
Unlike the UN
One region: Northern Virginia.
One generation: young leaders, 18 to 35.
Small, local, and hands-on.

Let's build this together.

LEAP is being founded right now. If this idea speaks to you, there is room at the table.
