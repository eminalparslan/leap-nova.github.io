LEAP Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2026

At a glance

  • Who we are: LEAP (Leaders Emerge and Partner) is a developing nonprofit community in Northern Virginia, primarily focused on young adults and emerging leaders. Participants must be 18 or older.
  • What we collect: Information you provide through our member intake form, such as your name, email, location, and committee interests, plus basic website technical data. Institutional applicants also provide information about their organization and partnership interests.
  • Sensitive information: LEAP does not request or intentionally collect sensitive personal information or payment information. Please do not include sensitive information in free-text responses. LEAP's programs are for adults 18 and older.
  • Why: To review applications, communicate with you, support committee and partner connections, evaluate our programs, and administer LEAP.
  • Who sees it: LEAP personnel who need it and service providers, such as Airtable, that support our operations.
  • Sale and advertising: LEAP does not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.
  • Emails: We may send application-related messages as needed. Optional LEAP news and event emails are sent only if you sign up, and each includes an unsubscribe option.
  • Questions and requests: Email hello@leap-nova.org with privacy questions or reasonable requests about your information. We consider requests in light of applicable legal, operational, confidentiality, safety, third-party, and record-retention needs.

1. Who we are and what this notice covers

LEAP (Leaders Emerge and Partner) is a developing regional nonprofit community that brings adults from Northern Virginia's many communities together to work on shared problems, with a primary programmatic focus on young adults and emerging leaders. LEAP is being formed by individuals and organizations across the region and is organized through leadership, advisory, and project committees.

This notice explains how we handle information when you:

  • fill out LEAP's member intake form, either as an individual or on behalf of an institution (a partner organization), and
  • visit our website, leap-nova.org.

When this notice says "LEAP," "we," or "us," it means LEAP (Leaders Emerge and Partner). When it says "you," it means the person filling out the form or visiting our site.

2. Information we collect

When you interact with LEAP, we may collect the information listed below. Some form fields are optional and may be left blank.

Everyone who applies

  • Full name (for institutions, the name of the contact person)
  • Whether you're applying as an individual or an institution
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • City or county
  • Committees you're interested in
  • How you heard about LEAP
  • What you hope to gain or bring, in your own words
  • Your agreement to LEAP's Code of Conduct and this privacy notice
  • Whether you'd like to receive LEAP news and event invites

If you apply as an individual

  • Your age range, used for demographic and program evaluation purposes rather than to determine eligibility above age 18
  • Your current status (for example student, working, or looking for work)
  • School or workplace
  • LinkedIn profile link
  • Whether you're interested in a leadership role

If you apply for an institution

  • Institution name and type (for example a university or student group, nonprofit, faith-based organization, cultural association, business, or government agency)
  • Your role at the institution
  • The institution's website or social page
  • How the institution would like to partner (for example co-hosting events, partnering on projects, sponsoring, providing space, offering mentors or speakers, or sharing opportunities)
  • Your confirmation that you're authorized to represent the institution

What our team adds

After you apply, LEAP's team may add an application status and brief internal notes needed for follow-up, program administration, committee placement, safety, or Code of Conduct matters. These fields are not part of the form.

Sensitive information

LEAP does not request or intentionally collect sensitive personal information, such as information about religion, race or ethnicity, health, immigration status, sexual orientation, government identification numbers, or financial accounts. Please do not include sensitive personal information in free-text responses.

Website and technical information

Our website is primarily informational. Service providers that deliver the site and form may process basic technical data, such as IP address, browser type, and visit time, to operate, secure, and improve those services.

Our website and form providers, including Airtable, may use necessary cookies or similar technologies to operate and secure their services. You can manage cookies through available prompts or your browser settings. Airtable's privacy information is available at airtable.com/company/privacy.

3. How we use your information

We may use your information to:

  • review your application and confirm that individual applicants are at least 18 or that an institutional contact is authorized to apply;
  • contact you about your application, next steps, and onboarding;
  • match you with committees, projects, and, if you say you're interested, leadership roles;
  • for institutions, explore and coordinate the partnership you described;
  • send application-related communications and, if you opt in, LEAP news and event invitations;
  • understand participation and evaluate and improve LEAP's programs;
  • keep LEAP safe and accountable, including enforcing our Code of Conduct; and
  • meet legal obligations.

We may also use information for compatible administrative purposes, to protect LEAP and others, to address disputes, or as required or permitted by law. We may ask for consent when appropriate.

4. Who can see your information and who we share it with

Inside LEAP. Leadership, volunteers, and committee leads may access information when reasonably needed for application review, program administration, committee placement, safety, or other LEAP activities.

Service providers. We use Airtable to provide the intake form and store application information. Airtable and other providers may process information to support functions such as hosting, forms, communications, and administration.

Partners and introductions. LEAP may share information within the organization to administer partnerships. We seek your consent before introducing you or sharing your contact details with an outside partner for an optional opportunity.

Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information as required or permitted by law, to respond to legal process, address misconduct or disputes, or protect LEAP or others.

Organizational changes. Information may be disclosed or transferred as part of a reorganization, merger, collaboration, or transfer of LEAP's activities, subject to appropriate consideration of privacy and applicable law.

5. Our data practices

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not share personal information for targeted advertising.
  • We do not request or intentionally collect sensitive personal information or payment details through the intake form. Please do not include sensitive information in free-text responses.
  • We do not ordinarily publish application information without permission, except where disclosure is required or permitted by law or needed for safety.

6. Emails and how to opt out

Application-related emails, such as confirmation or next-step messages, are part of the application process and do not depend on whether you sign up for news.

LEAP news and event invitations are separate and optional. We add you to that list if you sign up, and your choice does not affect application review.

You can stop optional news emails using the unsubscribe link in those messages or by emailing hello@leap-nova.org. Unsubscribing from news does not cancel your membership or application.

7. How long we keep your information

LEAP retains personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law for legal, administrative, auditing, or compliance purposes.

8. How we protect your information

LEAP uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to its size, activities, and the information it handles.

No system is completely secure. LEAP will address security incidents and provide notices when required by applicable law.

9. Age requirement

LEAP's programs are for adults 18 and older, with a primary programmatic focus on young adults and emerging leaders. Age above 18 is not an automatic eligibility criterion. The intake form is not intended for anyone under 18. If we learn that a person under 18 submitted information, we may take reasonable steps to remove it, subject to legal, safety, and record-retention needs. Institutional applications must be submitted by an adult authorized to represent the institution.

10. Privacy questions and requests

You may contact LEAP with questions or reasonable requests to access, correct, or delete information you provided, withdraw an application, or change your optional email preferences.

Email hello@leap-nova.org to make a request. We may need to verify your identity and will consider requests in light of applicable law and LEAP's operational, confidentiality, safety, third-party, and record-retention needs. A request may affect LEAP's ability to review an application or support continued participation.

11. Third-party services and links

Our form is provided by Airtable, and our website and emails may link to other services, such as partner, event, or social media sites. Those services handle information under their own policies, which you may wish to review before providing information.

12. Changes to this notice

LEAP may update this notice as its activities and practices develop. We will post the updated notice and revise the "Last updated" date. We may provide additional notice of material changes when appropriate or required by law.

13. Contact us

Questions, requests, or concerns? Email hello@leap-nova.org.
