We're each doing good work. What could we do together?

LEAP (Leaders Emerge and Partner) brings young leaders from communities across Northern Virginia to one table, to take on the problems we share, guided by the values we share.

Connect. Grow. Serve.

Why

We noticed something.

  1. The work is already happening.

    Young people across the region run service projects, mentorship programs, food drives, and dialogue nights for their own communities.

  2. Our communities have more in common than we think.

    We care about the same neighbors, face the same problems, and hold many of the same values. The overlap is real.

  3. But we rarely work together.

    When collaboration between communities happens, it happens by chance: one event, one introduction, and then it fades.

Social problems need social solutions. The problems we share are best solved by the communities that share them, together.

Our mission

To bring young leaders from Northern Virginia's communities together to find shared solutions to shared problems, guided by the values we all hold in common.

Our vision

A region where leaders of different communities know each other by name, call each other first, and regularly work side by side, so that each community is stronger because of the others.

How

Think of it as a United Nations for our region.

The nations of the world differ in language, history, and belief, and they keep those differences. But they share universal values and common problems. So they come to one table, stand up for what they hold in common, and take on together what none of them can solve as well alone.

LEAP is that table for the young leaders of Northern Virginia.

Like the UN
Members differ, and stay different.
They share values and problems.
Working together makes each of them more effective.
Unlike the UN
One region: Northern Virginia.
One generation: young leaders, 18 to 35.
Small, local, and hands-on.

Shared values guide shared action.

Any two communities can co-host an event. What's different here is that our shared values are the standing basis for every choice we make: who we work with, which problems we take on first, and which projects we run.

    Common ground we've found so far

    Values every community at our table already holds. This is a starting point, not a complete list: every community that joins helps us find more of what we share.

      Leaders at the table. Everyone in the work.

      At the table: leaders

      LEAP brings together the people who already lead in their communities. They meet as equals, plan together, and carry what they learn back home.

      In the work: everyone

      The projects those leaders plan are open to every member of every community, so neighbors who would never otherwise meet end up working side by side.

      We're not here to replace what your community already does. If you already run a food drive or a mentorship night, we don't want to compete with it. We want to give it partners, and a place to share ideas.

        What

        Committees: where shared values become shared work.

        Each committee brings leaders from different communities together around one shared problem, rooted in one shared value. We're starting with three on purpose. The rest should come from the communities who join us. (Names are working names.)
